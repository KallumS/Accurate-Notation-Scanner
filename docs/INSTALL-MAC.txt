Installing Accurate Notation Scanner on a Mac
=============================================

1. Drag Accurate Notation Scanner into the Applications folder (the
   shortcut beside it).

2. The first time only: in Applications, RIGHT-click (or Control-click)
   Accurate Notation Scanner and choose Open, then Open again in the box
   that appears.

   macOS asks because the app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer
   certificate. After this first time it opens normally with a double-click.

   If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal
   (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

       xattr -cr "/Applications/Accurate Notation Scanner.app"

   then open it again. That removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark,
   which is all the warning is about.

3. Drop PDF scores on its window (or on its icon in the Dock). Each one is
   read, checked bar by bar, and can be saved as MusicXML for Dorico,
   Sibelius, MuseScore, Finale or Noterator.

It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
